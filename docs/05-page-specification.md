# Luma Stay — Page Specification

## 01 — Navigation

Purpose: Provide orientation and access to major sections.

Elements:
- Luma Stay logo
- Rooms
- Experiences
- Dining
- Location
- Reserve
- Responsive mobile menu

## 02 — Hero

Purpose: Immediately establish the atmosphere and identity of the hotel.

Elements:
- Full-viewport photography or video
- Location label
- Editorial headline
- Supporting statement
- Primary reservation CTA
- Secondary exploration CTA
- Scroll indicator

## 03 — Hotel Introduction

Purpose: Introduce the philosophy and character of Luma Stay.

Elements:
- Editorial statement
- Supporting photography
- Architectural/location context
- Small metadata label

## 04 — Signature Suite

Purpose: Showcase the primary accommodation experience.

Elements:
- Large suite photography
- Suite name
- Description
- Room specifications
- Exploration CTA

## 05 — Architecture

Purpose: Communicate the architectural identity of the property.

Elements:
- Large architectural image
- Secondary image
- Editorial statement
- Material and design details

## 06 — Experiences

Purpose: Present the experiences associated with the hotel.

Possible experiences:
- Wellness
- Nature
- Private dining
- Local culture

The presentation should use editorial numbering and interaction instead of repetitive cards.

## 07 — Dining

Purpose: Present the culinary experience.

Elements:
- Restaurant identity
- Food/interior photography
- Editorial description
- Dining information
- CTA

## 08 — Gallery

Purpose: Create a visual storytelling sequence.

Elements:
- Asymmetrical image grid
- Large and small image compositions
- Image captions
- Category labels
- Hover interactions

## 09 — Location

Purpose: Connect Luma Stay with its destination.

Elements:
- Location statement
- Destination information
- Map-inspired visual
- Travel/access information

## 10 — Reservation CTA

Purpose: Provide a strong final conversion point.

Elements:
- Large editorial statement
- Primary reservation action
- Secondary contact action

## 11 — Footer

Purpose: Close the experience and provide utility navigation.

Elements:
- Luma Stay identity
- Navigation
- Contact information
- Social links
- Legal links
- Copyright
